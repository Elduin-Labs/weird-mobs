# 1.1.0

- The OKIE has a bar across the top of your screen now. It says OKIE and shows
  his health. He has 10 hearts.
- Poke an OKIE with a debug stick and he shrinks. Keep poking and he gets
  smaller and smaller, then pops back to normal.

# 1.0.0

The first weird mob: the **OKIE**.

It is a robot. It glides about on little legs you cannot see, it blinks, and if
you right-click it, it teaches you how to spell a word — one letter at a time.

It is friendly. It will never attack you.
